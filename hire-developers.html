FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I hire a developer?

Most clients get matched within 48-“72 hours. Niche roles like ML or blockchain may take up to a week. Once you approve someone, they can start in 1-“2 days.

What do I need to share to get started?

Just tell us what you're building, the tech stack (if you know it), seniority level, timeline, and rough budget. No detailed spec needed - a plain-language description is enough.

How much does it cost?

Junior developers run $20-“$60/hr, mid-level $60-“$100/hr, seniors $100-“$180/hr, and specialists up to $200/hr. That's a fraction of what an in-house hire costs when you factor in salary, benefits, and taxes.

Are there hidden fees?

None. We charge a flat platform fee that's disclosed upfront. No recruitment commissions, no onboarding charges, no exit penalties.

What engagement models do you offer?

Three options - dedicated developer (long-term), fixed-scope project (defined deliverables), or hourly (on-demand). Not sure which fits? Tell us your goal and we'll recommend one.

Can I hire a full team?

Yes. We can build you a complete squad - frontend, backend, QA, DevOps, and a tech lead. Start small and scale up whenever you're ready.

What if the developer doesn't work out?

We'll replace them free of charge within the first two weeks, no questions asked. You'll also have a real account manager - not a support ticket - to flag issues anytime.

How do I judge quality if I'm not technical?

Watch how they communicate. Do they ask smart questions? Flag problems early? Explain things clearly? Those are better signals than any coding test. We can also arrange a senior engineer review if you ever want a second opinion.

Do your developers use AI tools?

Yes - and that's a good thing. The best developers use tools like GitHub Copilot to move faster. What we test for is whether they can catch AI mistakes and deliver code that holds up in production, not just in a demo.

What stacks can I hire for?

React, Vue, Next.js, Node.js, Python, Go, Java, Flutter, React Native, AWS, GCP, Azure - and specialists in AI/ML, blockchain, cybersecurity, and more. If you don't see it, just ask.

How do you handle time zones?

We match developers to your working hours so there's at least 4 hours of daily overlap. They use your existing tools - Slack, Jira, Notion - and fit into your workflow, not the other way around.
